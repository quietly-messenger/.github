# Quietly

**A messenger that looks familiar and keeps nothing from you but your privacy.**

Quietly is a messaging app built around one simple idea: talking to people you care about shouldn't mean handing your conversations to a company. It combines the smooth, everyday feel of the apps you already know with a security model that doesn't cut corners.

## Why Quietly

Most messengers make you choose. Either you get a beautiful, fast, easy-to-use app and trust the company behind it with your data. Or you get "real" privacy and a clunky, confusing experience that only technical people bother with.

Quietly doesn't think that trade-off should exist.

- **Private by default.** Every conversation is end-to-end encrypted, always, not a special mode you have to remember to turn on.
- **No phone number, no email required.** Your identity on Quietly is just a username. We don't want your personal information, so we don't ask for it.
- **Your data, your call.** Message history backup is optional and encrypted, if you choose to enable it, only you can unlock it, never us.
- **Calls that respect your privacy too.** Voice and video calls default to a relay for your protection, with the option to connect directly with people you trust.
- **Not locked to one company's servers.** Anyone can run their own Quietly server, and it can still talk to the rest of the network. You're never stuck with just one provider.
- **Open, not just promised.** Quietly is open source. You don't have to take our word for how it protects you, anyone can look.

## Where things stand

Quietly is early - currently in active development, starting with Android. Our first milestone focuses on getting one-on-one messaging, encrypted calls, and file sharing rock solid. Group chats, other platforms, and more are already on the roadmap.

We'd rather ship something small and honest than something big and half-true, so expect the early releases to be focused rather than feature-packed.

## A note on trust

We can't ask you to just believe Quietly is secure, that's exactly the kind of promise every messenger makes. Instead, the code is open, the design choices are documented, and we'd genuinely welcome anyone who wants to look closely, ask hard questions, or point out where we got something wrong.

## Get involved

Quietly is a small, independent project right now. If the idea resonates with you, as a future user, a contributor, or just someone who wants to follow along - stick around. More details on contributing, testing early builds, and the technical architecture will follow as the project matures.
